Checkmarks appear in Gmail on Sent and in open threads. Hover for open history — updates every few seconds.

Opens count when the recipient opens the message in a Gmail thread (inbox/search), or in another mail app. Your own Sent folder views do not count. Opening Gmail without opening the message should not count; very short emails may still rarely prefetch.
